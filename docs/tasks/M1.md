## Next steps for M1-11 (balance)

The harness narrowed the remaining problems (details in `docs/balance_log.md`):

1. **Stalemates / Turtle mirror.** Turtle mirrors still reach escalation and run to 30:00. Gate defence (turrets + instant reinforcement + strong-age veterancy) beats the small armies the economy allows. Candidates: stronger escalation values; Artillery minimum range; a lower Bastion bonus; income/army-size changes (PLAN §9).
2. **Heavy spam** still beats the Hard Tactician (50% at n=20). Either Heavies are still too efficient per gold, or the Tactician's counter needs work (Heavies → more Blast Heavies and Siege).
3. **Rusher** is far below 40%: its units die to early turrets and feed the opponent XP. Its plan needs an early timing push before turrets, or turret costs need a look.
4. **Doctrine and pairing results flip at n=8** (Horde 0%↔100%). Mirror matches amplify tiny edges; judge doctrines at n ≥ 20 and consider adding cross-personality doctrine data.
