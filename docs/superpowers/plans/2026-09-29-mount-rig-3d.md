# Mount rig on the 3D core (stage 5) Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Build the mount rig and chariot of `2026-09-28-mount-rig-design.md` on `FkRig` and the camera yaw, following `2026-09-28-mount-rig.md` (whose tasks and code this reuses), so beasts turn with the same camera as the units.

## What changed from the 2D plan

1. **Legs are `Vector3` chains.** Each leg lies in the plane of its side (z = ±0.3 L, near toward the viewer) and is solved with `FkRig.reach3` and `ik3` (the in-plane pole is the plan's knee-forward, hock-back rule). `legs[leg]` holds the screen `Vector2` joints (as the plan) plus `p3`, the same in rig space.
2. **"Planted" is judged on the screen.** The ground scrolls at the beast's speed in screen px, but the camera squeezes the rig's x by cos(yaw), so the rig's stride is 1/cos(yaw) longer: the projected hoof slides back at exactly `pace` and stays put at any camera. The tests run at 0° and 25°.
3. **The centre plane is squeezed as a whole.** Body, neck, head, tail and cover are drawn under `_plane(view)` (x scaled by cos(yaw)); the legs are drawn from their projected joints, so a turned camera spreads the near and far legs by 2 · z · sin(yaw), about 6 px on a horse at 25°.
4. **Chariot planes.** Wheels (z = ±8) and panels (z = ±6) stand in planes of their own, so at 25° the near wheel and panel shift back and the far ones forward, with the driver on the centre line between. A wheel is a disc in the vertical plane, foreshortened to an ellipse; to roll without slipping on the screen it spins `pace / (radius · cos(yaw))`.
5. **The pastern folds through a smoothstep.** With the plain sine the front knee moved 3.03 px in one 1% step at lift-off, over the plan's 3 px "nothing snaps" limit.
6. **No far-wheel fudge.** The plan drew the far wheel at `(3, −1)` behind the near one; the planes now do it (nothing at 0°, 6.8 px apart at 25°).
7. **Other callers.** The scene's ambient running beast (`fx_layer.gd`) is drawn with the game's camera (`UnitArt.view_yaw`).

## Tasks

- [x] **Skeleton** (`addons/figure_kit/quadruped.gd`, `tests/test_fk_quadruped.gd`): bone lengths exact in 3D; planted hooves on the ground and not sliding on the screen at 0° and 25°; swinging hooves lift at least 0.08 H; four-beat order LH, LF, RH, RF a quarter stride apart; knees forward and hocks back; nothing snaps; the saddle where riders have always sat and bobbing; a mirrored beast flips every z; a turned camera spreads the near and far hooves by exactly 2 · z · sin(yaw) and only foreshortens the spine.
- [x] **Beasts** (`FkMounts.quadruped`, `rider_pose`, `mounted`): drawn on the skeleton, heads turning with the nod about the poll, the rider on the saddle joint with the beast's real bob.
- [x] **Chariot** (`chariot_joints`, `CHARIOT_ORDER`, `_plane`, `chariot`): wheel joints, rolling without slipping at 0° and 25°, the driver standing inside the car behind its near panel, no ground shadow for a figure standing on the car's floor.

## Result

- **Tests:** 194, 0 failures (180 after stage 4, plus 14).
- **Looked at:** every mounted role of all three races before and after, at 25°: the boar, war boar, horse, cataphract, elk, stag, ram, war ram, bear, elven steeds, and the three chariots. Knees and hocks fold, hooves plant, the heads keep their shapes, the rider sits on the saddle, the driver stands in the car with the panel over their legs.
- **Tried and rejected:** a straighter stance (`SLACK` 1.06 and 1.08). The hind hooves then float up to 0.8 px at the ends of their stance, and the test forbids that, so the plan's 1.10 stays. The small mounts (boar, ram) therefore stand a little crouched.
- **Not done:** trot and gallop (out of scope in the design); the siege machines' crews and golem and treant (their own rigs).
